I am a second-year PhD student at Kansas State University, advised by Dr. Doina Caragea. I work across machine learning and deep learning, including computer vision, transfer learning, self-supervised learning and federated learning. My current projects apply these to healthcare, where data is scarce and sensitive.

Before Kansas State, I completed my B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering at Chittagong University of Engineering and Technology and taught as a Lecturer at East Delta University.

Research

  • Infant behavior understanding Recognizing distress in infant patients from facial video, in a way that carries over to new patients and new hospitals.
  • Bedside monitoring Real-time tracking and 3D understanding of the bedside scene, toward systems that can flag medical device problems as they happen. Ongoing, as part of an NSF-funded project.
  • Privacy in medical imaging Sharing and learning from patient images and video without exposing who the patients are, from face anonymization to federated training across hospitals.
